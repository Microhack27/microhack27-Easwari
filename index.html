Guidelines

Eligibility & Rules

Everything you need to know before you register.

01 Who can participate?

  • Students from any department. Undergraduate and postgraduate students from Indian institutions and universities.
  • Students from Indian research centres.
  • Professional engineers and developers from India.
  • Researchers and faculty members from Indian institutions and universities.

02 Technical prerequisites

  • Microcontrollers: peripherals such as GPIO, UART, SPI, I²C, ADC, DAC, DMA, timers and PWM.
  • Firmware: proficiency in C or C++ for embedded targets; FreeRTOS is an advantage.
  • Debugging: breakpoints, serial logging, and oscilloscopes or logic analysers where needed.
  • Connectivity: BLE, Wi-Fi, Zigbee, CAN, RS-485, UART, SPI or I²C, depending on your problem statement.
  • Preferred, not mandatory: experience with MPLAB X IDE, MCC, XC compilers and Harmony v3.

03 General rules

  • Free registration. There is no registration fee.
  • Team size: 1 to 4 members. Choose your preferred problem statement while registering.
  • Bring your own sensors, controllers and peripheral components. Development kits will be provided only to shortlisted participants.
  • Set up one day before: install the software, compile a sample project and, if you are a shortlisted participant, verify that your development kit can be programmed.
  • AI usage. Participants must follow the official AI usage policy. Any restrictions on AI-generated code, content, or solutions will be communicated by the organisers.
  • Original work only. Plagiarised or pre-built solutions will be disqualified.
  • Build within the 36 hours. Work must be developed during the event window.
  • Carry a valid college or organisation ID card and your laptop with charger.
  • Respect everyone. Follow the code of conduct, venue rules and the organisers' instructions.
  • Fair play: the decisions of the organisers and judges are final.
  • Judging covers innovation, technical depth, working prototype, use of Microchip platforms and presentation.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about MICROHACK'27.

What is MICROHACK'27?

MICROHACK'27 is a National Level 36 Hours Hackathon organised by the MICROCHIP – Centre for Edge and Embedded Intelligence at Easwari Engineering College, Chennai. It begins on 25 February 2027 at 8:00 AM IST and winners are announced on 26 February 2027.

Who is eligible to participate in the hackathon?

Students from any department, undergraduate and postgraduate students from Indian institutions and universities, students from Indian research centres, professional engineers and developers from India, and researchers and faculty members from Indian institutions and universities.

Is there any registration fee?

No. Registration for MICROHACK'27 is completely free.

Do I need prior hackathon experience to participate?

Prior hackathon experience is not listed as an eligibility requirement. The technical prerequisites are listed in the Guidelines section. Further details will be announced soon.

Can we use AI tools during the hackathon?

Participants must follow the official AI usage policy. Any restrictions on AI-generated code, content, or solutions will be communicated by the organisers.
